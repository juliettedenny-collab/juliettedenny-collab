# Hi, I'm Juliette

I'm a founder and learning scientist. I build AI that helps people learn, and helps organisations change how they work.

I founded **Growth Engineering** in 2004 and built learning technology for Apple, HP, Cisco, Vodafone and the BBC, winning more than 120 awards along the way. In 2024 I founded **Zavmo**, an AI-native learning platform that teaches each person through conversation. I build it hands-on, working with AI every day.

## What I've built

- **Zavmo.** Twelve AI tutors and an orchestrator agent teach through conversation, with voice in and out. Two Training Magazine awards in 2026, for AI Innovation and for AI Coaching and Tutoring Tool.
- **Saraswati**, our own small language model. It only goes live when it beats existing models on learner outcomes.
- **An admin console** where a learning team builds a pathway just by asking for it.
- **zavmo.ai**, where 43,000 job descriptions became 33,000 role pages, each linked to routes through 2,825 qualifications from 130 awarding bodies.
- **A revenue dashboard** that joins search, clicks, payments and AI answer engine traffic, and **an outreach engine** that researches every organisation before it writes a word.

## How I build

- **Trust first.** Zavmo was certified to ISO 42001, ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 before we wrote a line of code.
- **Checks before anything ships.** Every change passes automated tests before it merges.
- **A person on anything that reaches a person.** A client approves every pathway. Someone approves every email.
- **Measure what changed.** Completion tells you who clicked. It doesn't tell you who can.
